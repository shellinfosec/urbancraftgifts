## 1. Pan-India Delivery Scope & Geographic Reach

Urban Craft & Co. coordinates reliable, nationwide delivery across India for standard and custom-branded corporate gifts. Our logistics network encompasses:

- **Tier-1 & Metro Cities:** New Delhi & NCR (Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad, Ghaziabad), Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune, and Ahmedabad.
- **Tier-2 & Tier-3 Commercial Hubs:** Chandigarh, Jaipur, Lucknow, Kochi, Indore, Surat, Coimbatore, Nagpur, Bhubaneswar, Patna, Visakhapatnam, and surrounding industrial corridors.
- **Special Economic Zones & Business Parks:** Corporate tech campuses, SEZ units, and industrial estates subject to verified gate security protocols.
- **Remote & Non-Metro PIN Codes:** Delivery feasibility, courier serviceability, and transit times for remote or special postal codes are confirmed during the quotation stage prior to order confirmation.

## 2. Dual-Stage Order Timelines: Production vs. Transit

Corporate gifting consignments involve two distinct phases: pre-dispatch customization and in-transit delivery. Timelines are evaluated and established for each order individually:

- **Phase 1: Production & Custom Branding Lead Time:** Begins only after written client approval of digital artwork proofs and confirmation of agreed advance commercial terms. Ready-stock catalog items without branding typically require 2–4 business days for QC and packing. Custom-branded hampers, laser-engraved sets, or bespoke presentation boxes typically require 5–12 business days depending on volume and branding technique.
- **Phase 2: In-Transit Courier & Cargo Time:** Begins upon handover to our contracted courier partner. Metro-to-metro air shipments typically deliver within 2–4 business days. Regional destinations, Tier-2/3 hubs, or heavy surface cargo consignments typically deliver within 4–7 business days following dispatch.

## 3. Freight Modes & Dispatch Options

Depending on order volume, consignment weight, budget preferences, and delivery urgency, we provide multiple dispatch options:

- **Express Air Cargo / Courier:** Recommended for time-sensitive deliveries, compact gift sets, executive presentation kits, and samples. Dispatched via tier-1 air express carriers (e.g., Blue Dart, Delhivery Air).
- **Surface Cargo & Bulk Logistics:** Cost-effective solution for palletized consignments, high-volume bulk sets, heavy drinkware cartons, and large luggage or bag orders. Dispatched through verified commercial cargo transporters.
- **Local NCR Dedicated Dispatch:** For large-scale consignments within Delhi, Gurugram, and Noida, dedicated direct-vehicle transport may be scheduled for direct dock delivery.

## 4. Freight Charges, Packaging & Local Levies

- **Transparent Quotations:** Estimated freight charges, fuel surcharges, and handling fees are itemized in your written quotation. Unless expressly agreed otherwise, freight is billed as part of the overall commercial invoice.
- **Freight-to-Pay / Client Logistics:** Corporate enterprise clients with existing contracted logistics accounts may arrange freight pickup from our New Delhi warehouse on a freight-collect basis by mutual agreement.
- **Protective Master Packaging:** All merchandise is packed in export-grade, heavy-duty corrugated outer master cartons with corner protectors, internal cushioning, bubble cushioning, and waterproof strapping to withstand handling during transit.
- **Local Permits & Entry Levies:** Any destination-specific municipal levies, state entry permits, or special SEZ gate clearances required by local authorities must be facilitated by the consignee.

## 5. Multi-Location & Work-From-Home (WFH) Dispatches

For distributed teams, national sales meets, and remote employee welcome kits, we support direct-to-home multi-address dispatches across India:

- **Validated Address Template:** Clients must provide a standardized Excel/CSV recipient sheet containing validated full names, complete residential street addresses, correct PIN codes, and active mobile numbers.
- **Individual Packing & Courier Dockets:** Each kit is individually packed, barcoded, and dispatched with an individual courier tracking docket.
- **Handling Surcharges:** Multi-address kitting, individual boxing, and individual tracking administration are quoted as an itemized service fee in the proposal.

## 6. Consignee Responsibilities & Failed Deliveries

To ensure timely, seamless delivery, the client or consignee is responsible for:

- Providing accurate, comprehensive address details (including building name, floor, wing, gate number, landmark, and correct PIN code).
- Ensuring a designated recipient or security team is available to accept delivery during standard business hours.
- Securing any advance gate passes or corporate security clearances required for delivery trucks entering private commercial IT parks or gated campuses.

If a delivery fails or is returned to our warehouse due to incorrect address information, prolonged consignee unavailability, or gate refusal, the client will be responsible for all associated return freight (RTO charges) and re-dispatch costs.

## 7. Consignment Tracking & Dispatch Alerts

Upon handover to the logistics carrier, our operations desk generates a formal dispatch advice email or message. This communication includes the carrier name, airway bill / docket number, number of master cartons, and an online tracking portal link so your team can monitor the shipment in real time.

## 8. Delivery Inspection & Proof of Delivery (POD)

To safeguard against carrier transit mishaps, please adhere to our receiving protocol:

1. **External Carton Inspection:** Before signing the physical delivery receipt or acknowledging digital OTP, visually inspect the exterior of all master cartons.
2. **Noting Discrepancies on POD:** If any master box exhibits noticeable tampering, crushed edges, tear, water damage, or damaged sealing tape, explicitly note "Received in Damaged Condition / Subject to Verification" on the courier's delivery manifest before signing.
3. **Photographic Evidence:** Take clear photographs or video of the affected cartons from multiple angles before moving or opening them.
4. **Internal Verification:** Unpack and inspect contents promptly. Any shortage, damage, or defect must be reported in accordance with our Cancellation, Returns & Refunds Policy.

## 9. Force Majeure & Transit Disruptions

While we partner exclusively with reputable national logistics carriers, transit timelines remain realistic operational estimates. Urban Craft & Co. is not liable for unavoidable transit delays caused by courier routing congestion, festive peak volume, severe weather, highway blockages, regional transport strikes, or civil restrictions. In the event of a known delay, our team will proactively follow up with carrier dispatchers to expedite clearance.
