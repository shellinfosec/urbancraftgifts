# 3. Shipping & Delivery Policy

### 3.1 Pan-India Delivery Coverage

Urban Craft & Co. arranges delivery of corporate gifts, customized hampers, and bulk merchandise to commercial and residential addresses across India. Delivery feasibility for remote, rural, or special postal codes is confirmed at the quotation stage.

### 3.2 Lead Times and Timelines

Because our orders range from standard ready-to-ship catalog items to complex bespoke corporate hampers with multi-point branding, timelines are calculated and agreed upon for each order individually.

1. **Production & Customisation Time:** Begins once the artwork proof is approved in writing and advance payment/commercial terms are completed. Standard branding takes approximately 5 to 12 business days depending on volume and printing technique.
2. **Transit Time:** Once dispatched from our facility, transit typically takes between 2 to 7 business days, depending on the destination city, pin code accessibility, and chosen freight mode (Air Courier vs. Surface Cargo).
3. **Delivery Estimates:** All dispatch and delivery dates shared prior to or during quotation are realistic estimates based on courier schedules. While we make every effort to meet agreed deadlines (especially for time-critical corporate events or festivals), transit delays caused by courier routing, extreme weather, public holidays, or regulatory checkpoints are beyond our direct operational control. We will proactively inform you of any unexpected transit delays.

### 3.3 Shipping Charges and Freight Terms

- Shipping charges, handling fees, and octroi/local levies (where applicable) are itemised in your written quotation.
- Depending on the commercial agreement, shipping can be prepaid and included on your tax invoice, or arranged on a freight-collect basis via your preferred corporate logistics partner.
- For multi-city distribution (such as direct-to-home delivery for remote employees), distribution fees and packaging surcharges are quoted based on the validated recipient address list.

### 3.4 Accurate Delivery Information

- Customers must provide accurate, complete delivery details including company name, recipient full name, exact street address with landmarks, PIN code, and active local contact numbers.
- If a delivery attempt fails due to an incorrect address, recipient absence, company gate refusal, or restricted entry, the courier may attempt re-delivery or return the shipment to our warehouse. Any additional re-shipping charges or courier return fees incurred will be billed to the customer.

### 3.5 Delivery Inspection and Receipt

1. **External Inspection:** Upon delivery, please inspect the external cartons before signing the courier delivery receipt. If a carton shows severe damage, crushing, tampering, or broken sealing tape, please note the condition on the courier's delivery manifest (or POD) and take clear photographs before opening.
2. **Internal Verification:** Please open and inspect the contents promptly after delivery. If any product is found damaged, defective, or missing, follow the reporting process outlined in our Cancellation, Returns & Refunds Policy.

### 3.6 Consignment Tracking

Upon dispatch of your shipment, we will share the courier tracking number, logistics partner details, and expected arrival date by email or messaging so you can monitor progress.

# 4. Cancellation, Returns & Refunds Policy

### 4.1 Enquiries Incur No Cancellation Charges

Website enquiries do not constitute placed orders. You are under no obligation and will incur no charge if you decide not to proceed after submitting an enquiry or receiving a quotation.

### 4.2 Cancellation of Confirmed Orders

Once a quotation has been accepted, an order confirmed, or a Purchase Order issued:

1. **Requesting Cancellation:** If you need to cancel or modify a confirmed order, contact us immediately via phone and email with your quotation or order reference number.
2. **Review of Order Stage:** Cancellation requests are assessed based on the production stage:
   - **Prior to Procurement or Production:** If materials have not been procured and no customisation or setup work has taken place, the order may be cancelled with a refund of advance payments minus any actual administrative or design-proofing costs incurred.
   - **After Branding, Procurement, or Customisation Commences:** Because corporate gifts customized with your specific logo, artwork, or bespoke packaging cannot be reused or resold, cancellation cannot be accommodated without deducting actual costs incurred. This includes the cost of procured stock, dedicated raw materials, completed branding, and packaging. You will remain responsible for all work completed up to the cancellation notice.

### 4.3 Policy on Returns & Change of Mind

- **Bespoke and Customised Goods:** We do not accept returns, exchanges, or refunds for change of mind, excess ordering, internal company budget changes, or aesthetic re-evaluations once an order is delivered in accordance with the approved artwork proof and written quotation.
- **Genuine Product Issues:** We stand behind the quality of our craftsmanship and materials. We will promptly inspect, rectify, and resolve any genuine issue where products delivered are:
  - Damaged during transit.
  - Manufacturing-defective or non-functional.
  - Incorrectly supplied (wrong SKU or mismatch in quantity).
  - Materially deviant from the specifications or artwork proofs approved in writing.

### 4.4 Reporting an Issue & Recommended Timelines

- **Prompt Reporting:** To ensure a swift resolution and allow timely claims with shipping carriers, we strongly recommend inspecting deliveries and notifying us within **48 hours** of package delivery.
- **Preservation of Rights:** This recommended 48-hour notification window is intended to facilitate rapid investigation and courier claim filing; it does not eliminate or prejudice any statutory consumer rights or legal remedies that cannot be lawfully excluded under applicable Indian laws.
- **Information to Provide:** When reporting an issue, please email info@urbancraftgifts.com with:
  1. Your order reference or quotation number.
  2. A clear explanation of the defect, damage, or discrepancy.
  3. Clear, well-lit photographs or short videos showing the shipping carton, shipping label, and the specific defective or damaged items.

### 4.5 Remediation & Resolution

Upon verifying a reported issue, Urban Craft & Co. will work with you to provide an appropriate, fair remedy, which may include:

1. **Replacement:** Re-producing and re-dispatching replacement pieces for defective, damaged, or missing items at our cost via expedited delivery.
2. **Repair or Rectification:** Arranging for corrective branding, repair, or component replacement where technically feasible.
3. **Credit Note or Refund:** If replacement is commercially unviable or stock is unavailable, issuing a proportional credit note or refund for the affected items.

### 4.6 Processing Refunds

- Approved refunds are processed via the original payment channel (such as direct bank transfer / NEFT / RTGS) within **7 to 10 business days** of formal resolution.
- Refunds apply strictly to the affected portion of the order and do not apply to accepted, undamaged items delivered in full compliance with the agreed terms.

### 4.7 Customisation Responsibility

Customers are solely responsible for verifying the accuracy of all digital artwork proofs, spelling, designations, corporate logos,Pantone/colour codes, and dimensions prior to providing written production approval. Urban Craft & Co. is not liable for errors present in the original artwork or proof that was expressly approved by the customer.

# 5. Publication Checklist & Legal Safeguards

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Notice for Business Review:** These policies are drafted specifically for Urban Craft & Co. as an enquiry-only B2B corporate gifting sole proprietorship. Before publishing these policies on the live website or incorporating them into commercial contracts, the proprietor should review the following checkpoints with legal and tax advisors.

### 5.1 Legal Identity & Sole Proprietorship Distinction

- **Trade Name vs. Legal Name:** "Urban Craft & Co." is a trade/brand name. Under Indian commercial and tax law, a sole proprietorship does not possess a separate legal personality from its proprietor.
- **Invoices & Purchase Orders:** Ensure that formal quotations, tax invoices, bank accounts, and statutory filings display the proprietor's registered legal name alongside the trade name "Urban Craft & Co." in accordance with GST registration details under GSTIN `07AEGPF3058P1Z7`.

### 5.2 Physical Address Disclosure Analysis

- **Business Request:** The business owner requested withholding the registered Okhla address from the public-facing website policy contact sections.
- **Legal Safeguard & Regulatory Check:**
  - _Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 (Rule 5):_ Requires e-commerce entities to display the legal name, principal geographic address of its headquarters and all branches, website name, and customer contact details prominently.
  - _Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules & IT Act, 2000:_ Recommends providing a physical point of contact for customer grievances.
  - _Recommendation:_ While an enquiry-only showcase without direct online checkout has a different risk profile than an automated retail checkout cart, Indian consumer authorities interpret commercial websites broadly. Prior to general public launch, legal counsel should confirm whether the registered principal place of business or a designated commercial liaison address must be disclosed in the footer or policy pages to ensure full regulatory compliance.

### 5.3 Technical & Privacy Alignment

- **Form Alignment:** Confirm that the enquiry forms, fields, and file upload modules on `https://urbancraftgifts.com` collect only the data types listed in Section 2.2 of the Privacy Policy.
- **Third-Party Services:** Verify that any third-party scripts (e.g., Cloudflare Turnstile, web fonts, Google Maps embeds) are accurately represented in your technical practices. Do not advertise proprietary encryption, analytics tools, or marketing cookies that are not actively deployed.

### 5.4 Statutory Consumer Remedies

- These policies explicitly preserve non-excludable statutory rights under the _Consumer Protection Act, 2019_ and the _Indian Contract Act, 1872_.
- Avoid adding rigid clauses (e.g., "claims after 24 hours will be summarily rejected") in operational communications, as such clauses can be deemed unfair contract terms under Indian consumer jurisprudence.
