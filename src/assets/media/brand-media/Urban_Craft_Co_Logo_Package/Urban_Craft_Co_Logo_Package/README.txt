URBAN CRAFT & CO - LOGO PACKAGE
================================
18 logo variations from your Logo System Board, in numbered folders.

BACKGROUND VARIANTS (file suffix)
  _transparent  transparent PNG, ready to place on any background
  _light-bg     on cream  #F8F6EE
  _dark-bg      on forest green #2F3A2E (text/lines switched to cream for contrast)

BRAND COLOURS
  Green #2F3A2E | Brown #8B5E3C | Sand #E8DDC9 | Gold #D4B483 | Cream #F8F6EE | Charcoal #2B2828

NOTES
  - All PNGs are 300 dpi. Full logos are ~3,300-4,900 px wide (4x your source file).
  - 08_Favicon_App_Icon includes favicon.ico + a sizes/ folder (16 to 512 px).
  - 15_Tiny_Icon has 16/20/24/32/48/64 px versions plus heavier-stroke outline versions for legibility.
  - 16_Social_Avatar: circle files have transparent corners; "_for_upload" files are square
    (Instagram/LinkedIn/Facebook crop to a circle themselves).
  - Not every logo has all three backgrounds: 10 and 17 are reversed-only; 12 (brown) has no dark version
    because brown on green has too little contrast.
